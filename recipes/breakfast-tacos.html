Chorizo Breakfast Tacos

Home

Description

These breakfast tacos are a great way to start your day. Most of this can be prepared ahead of time, so that you can build these in the morning for a quick breakfast. With bright flavors, from the pico de gallo and lime pickled jalapenos, combined with the richness of the soft scrambled eggs and chorizo, these tacos have a balanced taste that is sure to delight.

Ingredients

  • Street Taco sized tortillas
  • 1 lb ground Chorizo
  • 6 Eggs
  • 3 Roma Tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 limes
  • 3 Cloves of fresh garlic
  • 1-2 jalapenos, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp of Butter
  • 1 tsp of Sour Cream
  • Finely shredded cheese of your choice, for topping
  • Salt and Pepper, to taste

Steps

Before we can make the tacos, we have to get a couple of the toppings out of the way. These can be prepped the night ahead for a quick morning breakfast.

Pico de Gallo

  1. Finely dice the tomatoes, trying to leave out as much of the seeds and pulp as possible.
  2. Finely dice the onion
  3. Finely chop the garlics, and mix with tomatoes and onion in a bowl.
  4. Zest one lime into the mixture, and then add the juice of the lime.
  5. Salt and pepper to taste, the refrigerate until ready to use.

Lime-Pickled Jalapenos

  1. Add the thinly sliced jalapenos to a bowl.
  2. To the bowl, add the zest and juice of one lime.
  3. Salt and Pepper to taste, mix, and refrigerate until ready to use.

With those out of the way, the actual tacos are ready to be made.

  1. In a skillet, cook the chorizo until it is browned and crispy. Set aside.
  2. In a saucepan, crack your eggs in, and add the butter. Place skillet on a cold burner, and then set the burner to a medium low setting.
  3. Vigorously stir the eggs, while periodically removing from heat, so as to not curdle the eggs too quickly. Alternate having the pan on the burner and removing it until the eggs form a soft paste like constistency with small curds.
  4. Add the sour cream, and season to taste with Salt and Pepper.
  5. Lay out a tortilla, add a couple of spoonfuls each of the chorizo and eggs.
  6. Add the pico de gallo and jalapenos that were prepped earlier in your preferred anounts.
  7. Finally top with your shredded cheese of preference and enjoy!
